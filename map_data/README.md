# Offline map data

Generated by `python scripts/update_map_data.py` on 2026-11-07.

* `cities.json.gz`: GeoNames cities15000 worldwide plus populated places in Denmark.
  Names, alternative names, country, population and coordinates from
  https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/ . © GeoNames contributors,
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Data is filtered and repackaged.
* `countries.json.gz`: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 Countries exterior rings and
  country names, coordinates rounded to three decimals. Public domain:
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Source:
  https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector .

No live geocoding or map-tile service is used. City matches use country clues
when present; ambiguous or unknown names are skipped. This is a city-level
route overview, not a GPS track, road route, or estimate of delivery distance.
Small towns outside Denmark may not appear in the bundled gazetteer.

* `DejaVuSans.ttf`: DejaVu Sans for Danish city labels; see FONT-LICENSE.txt.
